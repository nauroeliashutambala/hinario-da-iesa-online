HINO 151 · Umbundo

Kuli ono yiwa yosonde

1. Kuli ono yiwa yosonde, Ya eciwa komanu vosi Lomola a Suku ongunga, Ha va moli omuenyo ko pui. Ndaño ame nda tungayala, Loku linga ovina via senga, Njuise vosonde yokameme, Oco ame njele pe. Ndocikokoto, Ndocikokoto, Njuise vosonde yokameme, Oco ame njele pe. 2. Vo futisa olosongo, Wa mola osoi kekulusu, Wa tala olohali via lua, Oku tu muisa eyovo. Songuile kono yaco a Ñala, Yi nombokaile osimbu yosi. Njuise vosonde yokameme, Oco ame njele pe. Ndocikokoto, Ndocikokoto, Njuise vosonde yokameme, Oco ame njele pe. 3. A Tate nda tungayala, Utima u kasi lahuku, Akandu wange a kusuka, Ovava ka a yelisa mbu. A Ñala kono yomuenyo Ndo songuileko lohenda: Sukule kutima a Suku, Oco ame njele pe. Ndocikokoto, Ndocikokoto, Njuise vosonde yokameme, Oco ame njele pe.
