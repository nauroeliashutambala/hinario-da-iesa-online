HINO 195 · Umbundo

Yola yola

Yola yola, ota memenako, Yola yola, momo Yesu o ku sole! 1. Yola yola, momo Yesu o ku sole, Eye wa kuliha upongo wove. Yola yola, momo u wa ku vala ndekamba Eye Isia ka pui. 2. Yola yola, momo Yesu o ku sole, Eye wakuliha ohali yove. Epute liove omanu va li yeva ño oku leha, Puãi Yesu o li yeva oku vala. 3. Yola yola, momo Yesu o ku sole, Eye wa kuliha ohali yove. Yola yola, momo eci ca veta upindi, Mbumbuangolo ka co sile. 4. Yola yola, momo Yesu o ku sole, Wa Oku ku ku kapa sokolola kolongolo eye ka pekela. viaye, Memenako momo eye o ku susumisa.
