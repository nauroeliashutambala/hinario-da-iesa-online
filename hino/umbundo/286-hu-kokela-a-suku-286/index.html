HINO 286 · Umbundo

Hu kokela, a Suku

1. Hu kokela, a Suku, Ocipepi, Ndaño ndukuelamba, Nala ohali; Utanya luteke, Njimba ocisungo eci, Hu kokela, a Suku, 2. Ndaño nda suvala Nda pua esanju; Ngenda lowelema Utanya mbu, Pokati kuteke Ku tuila ocinyi, Hu kokela, a Suku, Ocipepi. 3. Ndekise onjila Venda kilu, Mitise lombembua, Onguele sio; Oco ungende wosi Ngende londaka eyi Hu kokela, a Suku, Ocipepi.
