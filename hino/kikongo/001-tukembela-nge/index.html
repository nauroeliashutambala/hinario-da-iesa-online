HINO 1 · Kikongo

Tukembela Nge

Aleluya! Nkembo i waku; Aleluya! Ámem! Aleluya! Nkembo i waku; Utufulul'owau. 1. Tukembela Nge, E Nzambi o se, Muna Yisu wafwa, Wafuluka, mpe. 2. Tukemele Nge, Muna mwand'aku mpe, Watusongel'o Mvuluzi A wantu a nza. 3. Tukembela Nge Muna nzol'aku mpe, Watuvava, watukula, Watusongesela.
