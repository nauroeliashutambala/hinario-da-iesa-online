HINO 5 · Kikongo

O Nfumu'to okembelwa

1. O Nfumu'eto okembelwa Kwa wantu awonsono Kintinu kiezulu keyalanga, Kayala, yala, yala yal'ozo nza mpe. 2. Asumuki tunkembela, Oweto Mvuluzi, Imoyo katuvevwela: Kayala, yala, yala yakwayele mvu. 3. Owantu awanso ova nza Nukwama kukembela, Wa nkutu-bandu a Nzambi o Se, Kayela, yala, yala Yakwayele mvu. 4. Ambasi nutusadisa Yo yeno aveledi, O Yisu, ofwene kembelwa, Yo yala, yala, yokwayele mvu. 5. O Nzambi utulungisa Mu vu ki'aveledi, Twakwama kund'o Nvuluzi, Kayala, yala, yala, yakwayele nvu.
