HINO 15 · Kikongo

Ma masolandi

1. Ma masolandi ma Yisu, Yeno akwikidi, Nwa zangulanga edinga, Nwa sunda mu vita. Mu kati atwadimenga Nuzizilanga kata Nulunda Ntumi a vita Yandi i Nkubiki 2. Ma soladi ma Yisu, Ma yandinwaninu; Nwa vwate nvuatu miandi, Nwa diata ku ngolo, Nu kala mpe ye kasu, Muna lu sambulu, Mu ntangwa mpuku muni, Yandi nu zizila. 3. Ma soladi ma Yisu, Ku tambi ya ngolo; Mu vite yai ya mpasi, Ounu nsunda yo, Ô wau ya nvu ta nvy, Tu nkembela, nsundi, Tu nkembela Zambi eto Yo Klistu nvuluzi.
