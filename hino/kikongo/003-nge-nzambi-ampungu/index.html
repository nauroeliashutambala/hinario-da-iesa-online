HINO 3 · Kikongo

Nge Nzambi ampungu

1. Nge Nzambi ampungu, wavelela beni, Tukuyimbidila nkung'awankembo. Ngeye u Nkwa nkenda, Zamba a ngolo zingi, Ese, yo Mwana, yomuand'avelela. 2. Aya bena koko bekukundang'aka; Oku luse luaku bekukembela; Ambasi yo wantu bekuvuminanga; Ngeye i Nzambi ansigi kilu. 3. Nge Mfumu Nzambi walungilamwawonso; Kana vo kw'asumuki olembi moneka; Nge i Nzambi ansongi, osundidi owanso; Nge mosi kaka i Nzambi avelela. 4. Nge Nzambi ampungu a walakazi wingi; E vangwa yaku nkutu mpe yiyau ikukembela. Ngeye u Kwa nkanka, Nzambi a ngolo zingi, Ese, yo Mwana, yo Mwand'avelela.
