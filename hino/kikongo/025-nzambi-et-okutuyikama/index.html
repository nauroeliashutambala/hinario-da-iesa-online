HINO 25 · Kikongo

Nzambi et'okutuyikama

Tusa wanana! Tusa Wanana! Nzo a Yisu ova tumona yo, Tusa wanana! Tusa wanana! Kun'enzulu ova tukwenda ko. 1. Nzambi et'okutuyikama! Kanusimba, Kanufila Mu luludiku luani; Nzambi okala yeno wau tuvamba! 2. Nzambi et'okunuyikama! Muna mave okunukonda Kanuvan'o dia kweno! Nzambi okola yeno wautuvambana! 3. Nzambi et'okunuyikama! Wavo sumbula numona, Yavo vonza ikunuzieta; Nzambi okala yeno wautuvambana! 4. Nzambi et'okunuyikama! Vo nuyel'okunu wukana, Vo mu vimpi okunulunda; Nzambi okola yeno wautuvambana!
