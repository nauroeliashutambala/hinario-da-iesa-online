HINO 13 · Kikongo

Nani yandi zolele

Awei zolele, awei zolele, Kani mbvo kaka yandi lenda wa; Tata ye kune Zulu, tomene monanga Awei zolele, wiza owau. 1. Nani yandi zolele fwete sambila Luvuluzu lwa nkenda nufwete tondalo; Nga yeno tonda nulenda vuluka, Awei zolele kwiza owau? 2. Nani yandi zolele fwete kwiza wau E kia ki ekielo kia yangunka, lenda kota wau; Yandi Nfumu Yisu, zolele kotese, Wese zolele kwiza owau. 3. Nani yandi zolele fwete landa wau Yeno awonso nufwetw kwiza mpe Nani yandi zolele lenda kembela Wese zolele kwiza owau.
