HINO 14 · Kikongo

Wei fitakana

Wantambula owau ngina, Wantambula owau ngina, Mono yambi kansi o Yise wa fwa, Wantambula owau ngina, 1. Wei fitakana kwa Yisu, E nkenda zuku mbuenezo; Nga wangina yo mpasi za me, Wantambula owau ngina 2. Mu sele ngina ye ngolo nkatu, Luloloko lwaku umpana lo; Wa fwa ekuma kunkula mono, Wantambula owau ngina. 3. Ôkani diambote diau, Yengolo zame zilalanga mpe, Wanvuluza mono munkenda zaku Wantambula owau ngina.
