HINO 10 · Kikongo

E Nzambi umfinika

1. E Nzambi umfinika oko kwa Nge! Yisu unfilanga, wansandisa; Nkunga yayimbila, Nzambi yafinama! E Nzambi unfinika oko kwa Nge! 2. Wau nkangadilanga mu yayi nsi Nsangu yasamuna za Nvuluzi; Mu nzila nkwendela, Nzambi, yafinama! E Nzambi unfinika oko kwa Nge! 3. Nfumu za yayi nsi sambula do! Ankak, eyaku fu baveza yo. Yau bekunkandila: Nzambi, yafinama! E Nzambi unfinika oko kwa Nge! 4. Avo yabangama, usandisa Yatoma kembela yo fiaukila. Yisu unsimb'aka. Nzambi, yafinama! E Nzambi unfinika oko kwa Nge! 5. E Nfumu unsonganga nzil'aku do! Ku'zulu ifilanga, kiavilwa yo. Mbasi zandaulwila Nzambi, yafinama! E Nzambi unfinika oko kwa Nge!
