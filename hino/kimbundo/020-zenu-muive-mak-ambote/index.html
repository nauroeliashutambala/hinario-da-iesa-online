HINO 20 · Kimbundo

Zenu, muive mak'ambote

Tangelenu mundu oso Mak'ambote a Soba ia: Ngana Jezu, Mubuludi, A mu vuala kia. 1. Zenu, muive mak'ambote A Jezu mu Beleme; Kia mu vuala mona ndenge, Mon'a Nzambi phe: Te tubila tuenze ue; A mu sange, a mu beza, Emba fuma ie. 2. Mutu uala ni malamba, Iua haji maka ia: Tala muelu ua jikuka, Kristu ueza kia. Za, bokone ku ufolo Sakidila mbote je; Lelu a mu vuala kidi, Mubuludi ue. 3. Sakidila, sanguluka, U ximanejina diw; Paze, paze se zubilu, O dilau die. Balumuka, tu mu imbile, Ueza kia ni henda ie; Maku lelu a jikuka, Kristu kamba diw.
