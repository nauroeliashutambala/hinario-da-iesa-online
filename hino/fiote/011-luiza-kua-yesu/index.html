HINO 11 · Fiote

Luiza kua Yesu

Muntu, muntu, O, nadimbeti; Bwabu ntub´anlemvo yizidi, Tanbula Yesu, Nlemvo a Dise Ulenda kuvukisa 1. Luiza kua Yesu, lusampuka, Yambula tina! Lukubama. Nanguka, siseno fu biambi, Bilenda kubangisa. 2. Yambula wila nduenga zambi, Tina masiongi mankwa wambi, Landa Klisto nzila yayo; Luenga ye kubana. 3. Lumbu biabieto biakufama Ye tuna vioka ndimb´aluwa; Ndolo kwa Yesu tuatambula Moyo wamvu ya mvu
