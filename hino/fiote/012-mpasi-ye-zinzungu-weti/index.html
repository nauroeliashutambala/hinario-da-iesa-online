HINO 12 · Fiote

Mpasi ye zinzungu weti

Tanga nkenda, tangula zo, Tanga nkenda zeti sampanga, Tanga zaku ziwatambudi, Ngitukulu beni si wamwena zo. 1. Mpasi ye zinzungu weti yamusw´e? Tadidila Yesu Klisto, Nsadisi. Banzilanga nkenda zambi um mamo Zena kiese beni bu zitangwanga. 2. Banzilanga nsizi zikabedisa Zitu ye mayindu bikanatina, Mambu mandi um mamo mabombanga, Tanganga zinkumbu zikasidisa 3. Bu utala bantu ye bimvwama biau, Banzila zinkenda zikasilanga, Tanga zo zi ka zilendi sumbwa ko Ye minsendo miaku ku kayengele. 4. Tata mu dizulu weti vananga, Biobio bikazeyi bina tomisa, Sila ndwenga zandi vuvu um mamo Tuendi tanga nkenda zandi mvu ka mvu.
