HINO 18 · Fiote

Tukembela Nzambi

Tukembelele Nzambi, Wau katuzolele 1. Tukembela Nzambi Ku sulu evwandanga, O Mfumu eto ampwena, Mwawonso eyalanga; Kavang´e nsi ye zulu Nkwa ngolo, Nkwa zitu mpe. 2. Tukembelela Nzambi Okutuzolanga, Kayika yeto na´andi, Okutubanzanga; Katuma Yesu Klisto, Kafwa um kuma kieto. 3. Tukembelela Nzambi, Mambote evanganga; Nkwa ntim´avelela mpe; Kazola mambi ko; E ntima mieto miambi, I Yandi osukula mio. 4. Tukembelela Nzambi, Tunzolang´aka mpe. I muna nzil´azulu, Tuzol´a kwendela E Nzambi! Tata! Nkundi! Ku zulu utunata.
