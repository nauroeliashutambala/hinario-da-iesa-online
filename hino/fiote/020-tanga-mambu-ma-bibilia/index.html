Tanga mambu ma Bibilia

Mambu mamo tanganga mo, Mambu ma Mvulusi, Mambu mamo tanganga mo, Mambu ma Nzambi, Se. 1. Tanga mambu ma Bibilia, Mambu ma mvulusi; Inga, tuma mo zolanga, Mambu ma Nzambi, se. Matufwiti tanga, Mena mbote kwandi: 2. Yesu, Vananga kwa babo, Mambu ma Nzambi, Se; Nkwa masumu, nadimbeti, Mambu ma Mvulusi. Wiza tambuleti, Mena nkailu kwandi 3.
