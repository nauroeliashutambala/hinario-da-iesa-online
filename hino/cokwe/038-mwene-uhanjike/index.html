HINO 38 · Cokwe

Mwene, uhanjike

1. Mwene, uhanjike ngukwivwe, Ngunatate Liji lie; Usona nevwa kulivwa, Unguvwiselio, Mwene; Se ngunakupanjika, Yeswe Yena mungwamba. 2. Mwene, uhanjike ni yami, Hanga ngukunyik, Hanjika, nhukaule washi, Shimbo ujikolese; Kwa kulia mapanga je, Um chizulie cha liwe. 3. Hajika, yami mukepe, Kanda unguvulama, Unangunyingika wate, Um mbunge nahufwila, Lia kuyukisa mbunge, Hanjika, nguwahise. 4. Mwene hanjika, munguya Cheswe Liji mungwivwa; Ni chiseke kwononoka, Liji lieswe kukaula; Se ngunakupanjika, Mwe, hanjika kama.
