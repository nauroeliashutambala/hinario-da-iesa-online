HINO 17 · Cokwe

Se tunende nenyi

1. Se tunende nenyi, Liji lienyi pangu, Ni uhenya katutemwina; Twapemena Iye; Kanatwama hamwe, Twafuliela, twakwononoka. 2. Nyi chizulie chipwe, Hanji lelwa limwe, Kakuhumbulayo chawana, Soji ni kuyamba, Ni wakuhi woma, Twafuliela, twakwononoka. 3. Nyi yilemu yinji, Nyi yinyengo hashi, Iye kevwe matwononona; Nyi luyinda hano, Matuwahisaho, Twafuliela, twakwononoka. 4. Twakulishimwinya Ni kuwahilila, Mba mutuya hamwe ni Mwata, Chize mamba tute, Matutuma tuye, Twafuliela, twakwononoka.
