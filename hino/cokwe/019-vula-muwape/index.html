HINO 19 · Cokwe

Vula muwape

1. Vula muwape wa Zambi, Lino lie liji pundu; Kumakanoka he hashi Wa kuli Mwene kwilu. 2. Vula muwape wa Zambi, Atutumine, Mwene! Kuhuzuluka tunechi, Kulita ni Liji lie 3. Vula muwape wa Zambi, Tuma musono uno; Tunakulembe, Yehova, Nokesa vula mwilu.
