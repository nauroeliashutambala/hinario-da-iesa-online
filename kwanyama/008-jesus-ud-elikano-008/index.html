HINO 8 · Kwanyama

Jesus, ud elikano

1. Jesus, ud elikano, Ino efa nge. On'omukwafeli waveshe, Lia, kwafe nge! Jesus, Jesus, ino efa nge Ov'omuhekeleki wetu,L'u kwafa nge! 2. Koshipundi shoy shonghenda Pe ng' omobili yo, Ndishingone, Ov' Ependan Pe ng' eitavelo. Jesus, Jesus, ino efa nge Ov'omuhekeleki wetu,L'u kwafa nge! 3. Omulwefyo loy' alike Handi indile: Vlula oivad'aishe Yamomwefyo nee Jesus, Jesus, ino efa nge Ov'omuhekeleki wetu,L'u kwafa nge! 4. Ov'ofifiya yomwenyo Ov'omuholike. Ndge ndi he ku na momwenyo, Am' ohepele. Jesus, Jesus, ino efa nge Ov'omuhekeleki wetu,L'u kwafa nge! 5. Oto yandji ehafo shili, sh u hol nge. Omumwenyo ndi n'ombili Mwove amuke. Jesus, Jesus, ino efa nge Ov'omuhekeleki wetu,L'u kwafa nge!
